# Branding

The site uses the supplied `retrac.space logo.png`, `favicon.png`, and `favicon.ico` byte-for-byte in `public/`. The account mark in `public/brand/account.png` is copied from the supplied workspace asset `assets/retrac-space-auth-light.png`. Original workspace account artwork remains in `assets/`.

The wide logo includes transparent canvas padding. The header uses a viewport around that padding without stretching or redrawing the wordmark. Favicons retain their original sizes and proportions. No replacement logos, generated artwork, or improvised icons are used.

Colors follow the existing site's CSS: background #07060d and foreground #f3f0ff, with restrained lavender accents and thin borders. Secondary copy is brighter than the existing site's muted color for readability. Space Grotesk loads from Google Fonts at 400, 600, and 700, with system sans-serif only as a loading fallback.
